- Only nine runs exist (03:06–04:18 UTC). Seven were cancelled by the next push
  (`cancel-in-progress`), one is green, one was in progress. A burst of copy commits therefore
  produces no green result at all. The researcher's "nine of the last ten" overstates it.
- At the observed ~7.5 pushes/hour, the 2,000-minute GitHub Free budget lasts about one working day.
- OPS_SPEC §1.5 targeted ≤ 8 min wall and its T1 row already says py-db-slow moves to main-only if
  it measures > 12 min. It measures 17. The proposal is that fallback, made less blunt.

**The fix (about one hour, S effort, $0):** in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, add a paths filter so
py-db-slow runs only when `f1lab/`, `db/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `web/lib/queries/` or
`web/app/api/` change; add a nightly full run on `main` as the safety net for web-only changes that
break a model test; record the measured minutes in OPS §10 as the spec asked. Web-only pushes then
finish in ~5 min wall / ~8–9 billed minutes. Risk: a query-file rename the filter misses — hence
`web/lib/queries` and `web/app/api` stay on the slow-path list. Do it before the next burst of
pushes; it has no fan value, which is why it sits mid-table in §2(b) rather than in §1.

## §3 Rejected, and why

Every rejection any judge recorded, with the reason, so nobody re-proposes it without a new
argument that addresses the reason. "Reduced" variants that survive are named in §2(b).

1. **Saturday driver, Sunday driver [fan #9] — rejected as a section (two judges; honesty score 3).**
   Average qualifying position minus average race-pace rank (Russell 3.1 → 3.6, Stroll
   20.9 → 17.9, Leclerc 4.6 → 3.4) is measurable but attributes nothing: race-pace rank is car
   tyre behaviour plus traffic. It re-proposes the refused tyre-management skill (driver share
   0.03 %) in descriptive clothing with no new argument, and the researcher's own words are that
   the chart will beat the caption. Survives only as one row inside the driver page's "what we
   cannot measure" block.
2. **Crossed mixed model on pit-lane time [statistician #5] — rejected by all three judges.**
   Same claim ceiling as the median-delta version: `pit_stops` has no stationary (wheels-off)
   time, so a MixedLM plus a new table estimates the same session-fixed-effect quantity fan #6
   gets from a robust median with quartiles at zero pipeline cost. The exclusion rule (124 stops
   over 60 s; 79 NULL lane times) decides the ranking at the margin. "Haas 79.6 s raw mean" was
   not verified.
3. **Pit crew league [fan #6] — rejected as a team league (ranking judge).** Fans already have the
   DHL award's stationary times, which are the better number; entry-to-exit pit-lane time is a
   worse measurement of the same thing (Mercedes −0.48 s, Haas +0.55, Cadillac +1.00 verified).
   A per-race stop list on the race page is fine; a "fastest stop of the year" or a league is not.
4. **Precision curve per driver [statistician #8] — rejected as a chart (ranking judge).** Nobody
   asks "will the band tighten"; a 1/√n rule-of-thumb projection beside a rating invites
   over-reading of a number the app already caveats, and sd_island never shrinks with racing
   (MODE2 §1.5). One sentence on the driver page suffices.
5. **Scoring P(safety car) leave-one-race-out [statistician #10] — rejected as a page (two judges).**
   With 71 binary outcomes the Brier CI (~±0.03) straddles the base-rate Brier (0.242), so the
   panel's only honest output is "indistinguishable". Also needs new persistence: `preview_round`
   keeps only the 9 upcoming rounds. At most a one-line footnote on the weekend preview once past
   `p_safety_car` values are persisted — worth persisting them now so a real backtest exists in
   two seasons.
6. **RSS + JSON feed [operator #5] — rejected (two judges).** Near-zero audience; nine items then
   off-season silence reads as dead; and uncached it is the keep-alive OPS §5.1 rejected
   (100 hourly pollers ≈ 2,400 Neon wakes/month). Only conceivable after §1 #3 ships, and low
   value even then.
7. **Per-race CSV routes served from Neon [operator #6, on-demand variant] — rejected (two judges).**
   Direct egress exposure (the 5 GB cliff OPS §5.2 names; ~160 KB per race) and the licence line
   on timing data is undecided. Do not create the first public download before that sentence
